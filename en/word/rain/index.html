    Language notes

    • Japanese (雨)Sounds the same as 飴 (ame, candy); the two differ in pitch accent.
    • Spanish (lluvia)The ll sound varies a lot by region — it can sound like “y”, “ly”, “j” or “sh”.
    • Korean (비)The initial ㅂ is unvoiced, so it sounds closer to “pi”.
