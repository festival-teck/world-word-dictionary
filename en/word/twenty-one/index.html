    Language notes

    • French (vingt et un)Only numbers ending in 1 take et (“and”).
    • Spanish (veintiuno)Before a noun: veintiún (masculine) or veintiuna (feminine).
    • German (einundzwanzig)The ones come first (21 is “one-and-twenty”).
    • Korean (이십일)For counting things and for age, the native Korean number 스물하나 is used.
