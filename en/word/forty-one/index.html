    Language notes

    • French (quarante et un)Only numbers ending in 1 take et (“and”).
    • Spanish (cuarenta y uno)Before a noun: cuarenta y un (masculine) or cuarenta y una (feminine).
    • German (einundvierzig)The ones come first (21 is “one-and-twenty”).
    • Korean (사십일)For counting things and for age, the native Korean number 마흔하나 is used.
