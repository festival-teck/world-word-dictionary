    Language notes

    • French (soixante-quinze)Counted as 60 + 10–19. Belgium and Switzerland use septante instead.
    • Spanish (setenta y cinco)IPA shows Castilian Spanish; in Latin America [θ] (like English “th”) is pronounced [s].
    • German (fünfundsiebzig)The ones come first (21 is “one-and-twenty”).
    • Korean (칠십오)For counting things and for age, the native Korean number 일흔다섯 is used.
