    Language notes

    • French (jour)The daytime as a span is journée (f); noon is midi.
    • Italian (giorno)Also means “day”. Noon is mezzogiorno.
    • Spanish (día)Ends in -a but is masculine (el día). Noon is mediodía.
    • German (Tag)Noon or midday is Mittag.
    • Korean (낮)Noon is 정오; lunch is 점심.
    • Chinese (白天)Around noon is 中午 (zhōngwǔ).
