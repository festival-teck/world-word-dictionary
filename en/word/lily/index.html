    Language notes

    • French (lys)Also spelled lis. The flower of the French royal emblem, the fleur-de-lis.
    • Italian (giglio)The flower on Florence’s coat of arms.
    • Spanish (lirio)A white lily is also called azucena.
    • Korean (백합)Pronounced [배캅] (baekap).
