    Language notes

    • English (heart)“Heart” is also used for the mind and feelings.
    • French (cœur)Cœur also means heart in the emotional sense.
    • Italian (cuore)Cuore also means heart in the emotional sense.
    • Spanish (corazón)Corazón also means heart in the emotional sense.IPA shows Castilian Spanish; in Latin America [θ] (like English “th”) is pronounced [s].
