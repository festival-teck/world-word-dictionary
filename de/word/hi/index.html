    Language notes

    • English (fire)Also means “to dismiss from a job” and “to shoot”.
    • French (feu)As an adjective, feu also means “late, deceased” (feu mon père, my late father).
    • Korean (불)Also used for “light” or “the lights” (불을 켜다, to turn on the light).
    • Chinese (火)In slang it means “popular” or “going viral”.
