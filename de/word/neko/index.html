    Japanese is romanized in Hepburn, Chinese in pinyin, Korean in Revised Romanization; the rest in IPA (Castilian Spanish). Notation

    Check before using it as a name

    • French “chat”: The feminine chatte is vulgar slang for female genitalia — avoid it for names and products.

    Language notes

    • Italian (gatto)A female cat is gatta.
    • Spanish (gato)A female cat is gata. Gato also means a car jack.
