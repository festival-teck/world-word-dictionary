    Language notes

    • English (lightning)Not to be confused with “lightening” (making lighter), which is spelled differently.
    • French (éclair)The pastry éclair has the same name.
    • Italian (fulmine)A flash without a strike is lampo.
    • Spanish (relámpago)A lightning strike hitting the ground is rayo.
    • Korean (번개)In slang it also means an impromptu meetup (번개 모임).
