    Language notes

    • Japanese (こんにちは)Written with は but pronounced wa.
    • Italian (buongiorno)Casually, ciao.
    • Spanish (hola)As an afternoon greeting, buenas tardes.
    • Korean (안녕하세요)Used at any time of day; to close friends, 안녕.
