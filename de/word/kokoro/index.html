    Language notes

    • English (heart)For heart in the sense of thinking or spirit, “mind” is used.
    • French (cœur)œ is a ligature of o and e.
    • Spanish (corazón)Also means the heart as an organ.IPA shows Castilian Spanish; in Latin America [θ] (like English “th”) is pronounced [s].
    • Korean (마음)The organ and the ♥ shape use different words: 심장 and 하트.
