    Japanese is romanized in Hepburn, Chinese in pinyin, Korean in Revised Romanization; the rest in IPA (Castilian Spanish). Notation

    Check before using it as a name

    • Spanish “conejo”: In Spain and elsewhere it can be vulgar slang for female genitalia — be careful using it as a name.
    • Chinese “兔子”: Sometimes used as a slur against gay men.

    Language notes

    • English (rabbit)In children’s speech or as a pet name, “bunny”.
    • French (lapin)Mon lapin is used as a term of endearment.
