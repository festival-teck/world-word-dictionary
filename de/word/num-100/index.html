    Language notes

    • English (one hundred)Also “a hundred”.
    • Spanish (cien)Exactly 100 is cien; from 101 it becomes ciento (ciento uno).IPA shows Castilian Spanish; in Latin America [θ] (like English “th”) is pronounced [s].
    • Chinese (一百)It is said with 一 (“one hundred”); 一 changes tone and is pronounced yìbǎi.
