    Language notes

    • English (arm)The plural “arms” can also mean weapons.
    • French (bras)The s is silent.
    • Italian (braccio)The plural braccia is treated as feminine.
    • Spanish (brazo)IPA shows Castilian Spanish; in Latin America [θ] (like English “th”) is pronounced [s].
    • Korean (팔)팔 is also the number 8 (八).
    • Chinese (胳膊)In writing, 手臂 (shǒubì).
