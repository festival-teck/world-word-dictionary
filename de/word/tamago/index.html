    Japanese is romanized in Hepburn, Chinese in pinyin, Korean in Revised Romanization; the rest in IPA (Castilian Spanish). Notation

    Check before using it as a name

    • Spanish “huevo”: The plural huevos is slang for testicles.

    Language notes

    • French (œuf)The plural œufs is pronounced [ø].
    • Italian (uovo)The plural is uova (treated as feminine).
    • Korean (달걀)Also 계란 (gyeran).
