    Japanese is romanized in Hepburn, Chinese in pinyin, Korean in Revised Romanization; the rest in IPA (Castilian Spanish). Notation

    Check before using it as a name

    • Spanish “zorro”: The feminine zorra is a strong insult toward women.

    Language notes

    • English (fox)The adjective “foxy” means cunning or sexy.
    • Spanish (zorro)IPA shows Castilian Spanish; in Latin America [θ] (like English “th”) is pronounced [s].
    • Korean (여우)In slang it also means a sly, flirtatious woman.
    • Chinese (狐狸)狐狸精 is an insult for a woman who seduces men.
