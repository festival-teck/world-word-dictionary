    Language notes

    • English (word)A whole language is “language”.
    • French (mot)“Language” is langue.
    • German (Wort)The plural splits in meaning: Wörter (words) and Worte (spoken words, remarks).
    • Korean (말)말 also means “horse”.
    • Chinese (词语)Spoken words are 话 (huà); “language” is 语言.
