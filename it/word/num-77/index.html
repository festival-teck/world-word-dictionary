    Language notes

    • French (soixante-dix-sept)Counted as 60 + 10–19. Belgium and Switzerland use septante instead.
    • German (siebenundsiebzig)The ones come first (21 is “one-and-twenty”).
    • Korean (칠십칠)For counting things and for age, the native Korean number 일흔일곱 is used.
