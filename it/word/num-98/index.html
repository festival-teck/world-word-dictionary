    Language notes

    • French (quatre-vingt-dix-huit)Counted as four times twenty plus 10–19. Belgium and Switzerland use nonante instead.
    • German (achtundneunzig)The ones come first (21 is “one-and-twenty”).
    • Korean (구십팔)For counting things and for age, the native Korean number 아흔여덟 is used.
