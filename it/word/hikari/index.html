    Language notes

    • Japanese (光)Also a common given name in Japan.
    • English (light)“Light” also means “not heavy” and “bright”.
    • Spanish (luz)Also a common female name, Luz.IPA shows Castilian Spanish; in Latin America [θ] (like English “th”) is pronounced [s].
    • Korean (빛)When followed by a vowel, the final ㅊ sound appears: 빛이 (bichi).
