    Language notes

    • Japanese (零)Zero (ゼロ, zero) is also very common.
    • English (zero)In phone and room numbers it is often read “oh”.
    • Spanish (cero)IPA shows Castilian Spanish; in Latin America [θ] (like English “th”) is pronounced [s].
    • Korean (영)In phone numbers it is also read 공 (gong).
