    Japanese is romanized in Hepburn, Chinese in pinyin, Korean in Revised Romanization; the rest in IPA (Castilian Spanish). Notation

    Check before using it as a name

    • Korean “사과”: 사과 also means “apology”. The fruit word is said to come from the Sino-Korean 沙果.

    Language notes

    • French (pomme)A potato is pomme de terre, “apple of the earth”.
    • Spanish (manzana)Also means a city block.IPA shows Castilian Spanish; in Latin America [θ] (like English “th”) is pronounced [s].
    • Chinese (苹果)Apple the company is also 苹果.
