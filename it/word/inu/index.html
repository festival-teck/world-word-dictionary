    Japanese is romanized in Hepburn, Chinese in pinyin, Korean in Revised Romanization; the rest in IPA (Castilian Spanish). Notation

    Check before using it as a name

    • French “chien”: The feminine chienne is a strong insult when aimed at a person, especially a woman.
    • Korean “개”: In slang, 개- is a crude intensifier meaning “really”. A puppy is 강아지 (gangaji).
    • Chinese “狗”: Used as an insult when aimed at a person.

    Language notes

    • Spanish (perro)The rr is trilled, distinguishing it from pero (but).
