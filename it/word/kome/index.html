    Language notes

    • Japanese (米)Cooked rice, or a meal, is ご飯 (gohan).
    • Spanish (arroz)IPA shows Castilian Spanish; in Latin America [θ] (like English “th”) is pronounced [s].
    • German (Reis)Not to be confused with Reise (journey).
    • Korean (쌀)Cooked rice is 밥 (bap).
    • Chinese (米)Cooked rice is 米饭 (mǐfàn).
