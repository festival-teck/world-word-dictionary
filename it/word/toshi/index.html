    Japanese is romanized in Hepburn, Chinese in pinyin, Korean in Revised Romanization; the rest in IPA (Castilian Spanish). Notation

    Check before using it as a name

    • Spanish “año”: Written without the tilde, ano means “anus” — make sure the ñ is kept.

    Language notes

    • Japanese (年)Read nen (ねん) when counting years.
    • French (an)A year as a span of time is année (f).
    • Korean (해)해 also means “sun”. When counting years, 년 (nyeon) is used.
