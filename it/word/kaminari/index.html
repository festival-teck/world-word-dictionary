    Language notes

    • English (thunder)“Thunder” is the sound; the flash is “lightning”.
    • French (tonnerre)The flash of lightning is éclair.
    • German (Donner)The root of Donnerstag (Thursday, the thunder god’s day).
    • Korean (천둥)The flash of lightning is 번개 (beongae).
    • Chinese (雷)In slang, 雷 (léi) also means “shockingly awful”.
