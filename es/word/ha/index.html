    Language notes

    • Japanese (葉)Sounds the same as 歯 (ha, tooth).
    • English (leaf)The plural is “leaves”.
    • French (feuille)Also means a sheet of paper.
    • Italian (foglia)A sheet of paper is foglio (masculine) — a different form.
    • German (Blatt)Also means a sheet of paper.
    • Korean (잎)A tree leaf is 나뭇잎 (namunnip).
