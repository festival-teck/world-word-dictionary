    Language notes

    • Japanese (花)Sounds the same as 鼻 (hana, nose); the two differ only in pitch accent.
    • English (flower)“Flour”, which sounds the same, is ground grain.
    • German (Blume)Blossoms on trees are Blüte, not Blume.
    • Korean (꽃)When followed by a vowel, the final ㅊ sound appears: 꽃이 (kkochi).
    • Chinese (花)Also means “to spend (money)” and “patterned”.
