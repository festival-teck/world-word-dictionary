    Language notes

    • Japanese (柿)Sounds the same as 牡蠣 (kaki, oyster).
    • English (persimmon)“Kaki” is sometimes understood too.
    • French (kaki)Also the color khaki.
    • Italian (cachi)Same form in the singular and plural.
    • Korean (감)감 also means “feeling” (感) and “intuition” (勘).
