    Language notes

    • French (quatre-vingt-un)Counted as four times twenty plus a number.
    • German (einundachtzig)The ones come first (21 is “one-and-twenty”).
    • Korean (팔십일)For counting things and for age, the native Korean number 여든하나 is used.
