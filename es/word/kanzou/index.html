    Language notes

    • French (foie)As in foie gras. Pronounced the same as fois (times).
    • Italian (fegato)Avere fegato means “to have guts”.
    • German (Leber)Shares its origin with English “liver”.
    • Korean (간)A different 간 also means “seasoning, saltiness”.
    • Chinese (肝脏)The single character 肝 (gān) is also used.
