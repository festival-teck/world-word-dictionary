    Language notes

    • Japanese (おはよう)The polite form is おはようございます (ohayō gozaimasu).
    • French (bonjour)French has no special morning greeting; bonjour is used all day.
    • Italian (buongiorno)Buongiorno is used until early afternoon.
    • Korean (좋은 아침)A literal “good morning”; 안녕하세요 is the usual greeting even in the morning.
    • Chinese (早上好)Casually, just 早 (zǎo).
