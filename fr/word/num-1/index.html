    Language notes

    • Italian (uno)Before a noun: un or uno (masculine), una (feminine).
    • Spanish (uno)Before a noun: un (masculine) or una (feminine).
    • German (eins)Counting, it is eins; before a noun it becomes ein or eine.
    • Korean (일)For counting things and for age, the native Korean number 하나 is used.
    • Chinese (一)In phone and room numbers it is often read yāo.
