    Language notes

    • Japanese (空)Also a common given name in Japan.
    • Italian (cielo)Also means “heaven”.
    • Spanish (cielo)Also means “heaven”, and mi cielo is an affectionate “my darling”.IPA shows Castilian Spanish; in Latin America [θ] (like English “th”) is pronounced [s].
    • German (Himmel)Also means “heaven”.
    • Korean (하늘)Also means “heaven”, and is used as a given name in Korea.
    • Chinese (天空)The single character 天 (tiān) can also mean sky.
