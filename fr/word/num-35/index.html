    Language notes

    • Spanish (treinta y cinco)IPA shows Castilian Spanish; in Latin America [θ] (like English “th”) is pronounced [s].
    • German (fünfunddreißig)The ones come first (21 is “one-and-twenty”).
    • Korean (삼십오)For counting things and for age, the native Korean number 서른다섯 is used.
