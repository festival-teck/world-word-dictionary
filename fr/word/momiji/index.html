    Language notes

    • Japanese (紅葉)Also read kōyō (こうよう), meaning autumn foliage in general. Momiji also refers to the Japanese maple.
    • English (autumn leaves)In American English also “fall foliage”. The tree itself is a maple.
    • Italian (foglie autunnali)A plural expression.
    • Spanish (hojas de otoño)A plural expression.
    • German (Herbstlaub)Literally “autumn foliage”.
    • Korean (단풍)The maple tree is 단풍나무.
