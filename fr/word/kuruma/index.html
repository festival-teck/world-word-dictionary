    Language notes

    • Italian (macchina)Also means “machine”. Auto also means car.
    • Spanish (coche)In Latin America, carro or auto is more common.
    • Korean (자동차)In speech, just 차 (cha) is common.
    • Chinese (汽车)Unlike Japanese 汽車 (steam train), Chinese 汽车 means a car.
