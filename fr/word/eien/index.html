    Language notes

    • Japanese (永遠)Used both as a noun (eternity) and adverbially as 永遠に (eien ni, forever).
    • English (eternity)The adjective is “eternal”.
    • Italian (eternità)Stress falls on the final à.
    • Spanish (eternidad)The final d is barely audible.
    • Chinese (永恒)The adverb “forever” is 永远 (yǒngyuǎn).
