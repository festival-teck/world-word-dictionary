    Language notes

    • English (clock)A wristwatch is a “watch”.
    • German (Uhr)Also used for telling the time (“… o’clock”).
    • Chinese (钟)送钟 (giving a clock) sounds like 送终 (attending someone’s death), so clocks are taboo as gifts. A watch is 表 (biǎo).
