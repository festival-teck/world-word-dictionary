    Language notes

    • Japanese (さようなら)Sounds rather final; friends usually say じゃあね (jā ne) or またね (mata ne).
    • Italian (arrivederci)Polite; to friends, ciao.
    • Korean (안녕히 가세요)Said to someone who is leaving; the person leaving says 안녕히 계세요.
